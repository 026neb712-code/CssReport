A computer is an electronic device that takes input, processes data, stores information, and produces an output.It consists of major hardware parts like the central processing unit (CPU), monitor, keyboard, and mouse. Today, computers are essential tools used in schools, offices, hospitals, and homes to make daily tasks faster and easier. They help people communicate globally through the internet, manage business accounts, and solve complex problems in seconds.
