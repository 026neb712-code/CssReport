A computer is an electronic device that takes input, processes data, stores information, and produces an output.It consists of major hardware parts like the central processing unit (CPU), monitor, keyboard, and mouse. Charles Babbage is widely known as the father of computers for laying the foundational concepts of calculating machines. Today, computers are essential tools used in schools, offices, hospitals, and homes to make daily tasks faster and easier. They help people communicate globally through the internet, manage business accounts, and solve complex problems in seconds.
